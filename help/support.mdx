The GoodBoyRobot team — and Byte — are here to help. Whether you have a quick question or a thorny integration problem, this page explains the support channels available to you, what to expect from each one, and how to get the fastest response possible. ## Support Tiers

GoodBoyRobot offers three support tiers based on your plan. All tiers have access to the documentation and community resources.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Community" icon="users">
    **Available on:** Free plan

    Access the GoodBoyRobot Discord server and public documentation. Community members and the team actively monitor Discord and answer questions on a best-effort basis. Ideal for exploration and non-critical questions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standard" icon="headset">
    **Available on:** Paid plans

    Direct email support with a **next business day** response SLA. Reach the support team at [help@goodboyrobot.com](mailto:help@goodboyrobot.com) or through the in-app chat widget. Best for production workloads that need reliable, timely help.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Priority" icon="bolt">
    **Available on:** Enterprise plans

    Everything in Standard, plus a **4-hour response SLA** (during business hours) and a dedicated Slack channel with a named account engineer. Designed for mission-critical deployments. Contact [sales@goodboyrobot.com](mailto:sales@goodboyrobot.com) to discuss enterprise pricing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Reach Support

Choose the channel that best fits your situation and plan.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email">
    Send a message to **[help@goodboyrobot.com](mailto:help@goodboyrobot.com)** for any support request. Email support is available to Standard and Priority plan customers and is monitored Monday through Friday during business hours (9 AM – 6 PM ET).

    Include the information listed in the [What to Include](#what-to-include-in-a-support-request) section below to help the team resolve your issue as quickly as possible.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In-App Chat">
    Click the **chat icon in the bottom-right corner** of any page in the GoodBoyRobot dashboard to open the support chat. This connects you to the same support team as email and is the fastest way to start a conversation during business hours.

    Chat is available on paid plans. Free plan users will be directed to the community Discord.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Discord Community">
    Join the **GoodBoyRobot Discord** at [discord.gg/goodboyrobot](https://discord.gg/goodboyrobot) to connect with other developers, share ideas, ask questions, and get help from the community. The `#support` channel is monitored by team members, though responses are on a best-effort basis.

    Discord is open to everyone — no paid plan required.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What to Include in a Support Request

Providing the right context upfront helps the support team skip the back-and-forth and get straight to a resolution. Include the following in every request:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your account email">
    The email address associated with your GoodBoyRobot account, so the team can locate your workspace quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your environment">
    Specify which environment the issue occurred in — `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Issues sometimes behave differently across environments, so this detail matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The request ID">
    Every API response includes an `x-request-id` header. Copy the value from the failing request and include it in your report. This is the single most useful piece of information for the team to trace exactly what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The full error message">
    Paste the complete error response body — not just the status code. Include any stack traces if you are reporting an SDK issue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always grab the `x-request-id` value from your API response headers before closing the request. It acts like a tracking number — the support team can use it to pull up the full server-side log of that exact request in seconds, drastically reducing diagnosis time.
</Tip>

## Response Time SLAs

| Support Tier | First Response Time       | Channel                        |
|--------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Community    | Best effort               | Discord                        |
| Standard     | Next business day         | Email, In-App Chat             |
| Priority     | 4 hours (business hours)  | Email, In-App Chat, Slack      |

Business hours are **Monday – Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM ET**, excluding public holidays. Priority SLAs apply during business hours; after-hours requests will receive a response at the start of the next business day.
